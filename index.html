Be honest with yourself

Is this for you?

Better to know now than after you have joined.

This is for you if

  • You make your own content and it eats hours every week
  • You post and get very little back, and you are not sure why
  • You answer the same DMs and comments again and again
  • You want AI to do the repetitive work without losing your voice
  • You run a small business, freelance, or build a personal brand

This is not for you if

  • You want a button that posts generic content for you
  • You are after overnight virality with no testing
  • You want someone else to run your accounts for you
  • You already have a large in house social team and tools

What is Ampvox Academy?

Ampvox Academy is a self paced online course that teaches creators and small businesses how to use AI to plan, create, automate and measure their social media, so they can make their own content with far less manual work.

Who is it for?

Creators, freelancers and small business owners who run their own social media and want to save time without losing their own voice. It also suits brands without a face that want to build an AI character.

What will I learn?

Twenty short modules in six parts: finding out why your posts flop, writing hooks, planning content, an optional AI character, automating DMs and email, and knowing what works.

Do I need experience with AI tools?

No. Every module starts from the basics and builds up, so you can follow along even if you have never opened an AI tool.

Do I have to use AI avatars?

No. The avatar modules are optional. Most of the course is about systems that speed up your own content, so you can show up yourself and still save hours.

Which tools will I need?

Most lessons use free or low cost tools. Each module lists exactly what it uses before you start.

How long does it take?

It is self paced. Go through it in a weekend or spread it across a month, whatever suits you.

Who is behind it?

Ampvox, a social media agency that runs strategy, AI content and creator management for real clients. The course teaches the same methods the agency uses.

When does it open and what is the launch discount?

The course opens on 1 November 2026. Everyone on the early access list gets a launch discount and hears about it by email first.
